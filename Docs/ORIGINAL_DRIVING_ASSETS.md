# Original assets added in the cinematic driving pass

The 24 engine-layer WAVs, horn, shift, blowoff, passby, scrape, heavy_impact, explosion, fire, damage_warning and road_noise were synthesized specifically for Velocity Rush 3D with Tools/generate_driving_audio.py. They contain no downloaded commercial recordings or samples. The four Morning/Evening/Dusk/Night HDR panoramas were generated mathematically by Tools/generate_time_of_day.py. Medium traffic GLBs are generated from the existing original fictional traffic mesh generator by Tools/generate_traffic_medium.py. The cockpit and cosmetic damage effects are original procedural geometry.

NumPy/SciPy are authoring dependencies only; no Python libraries are shipped as Android runtime dependencies. These assets may be used and modified with this project. The audio remains synthesized; sound quality on real hardware has not been listening-tested here.

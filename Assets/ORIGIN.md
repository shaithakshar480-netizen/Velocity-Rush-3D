# Asset provenance

The six Phase 3 WAV files are original deterministic mathematical synthesis from Tools/generate_phase3_audio.py. No downloaded engine/game recordings are used. Exhaust is an original procedural shader with reusable quads; speed streaks are code-drawn lines. Traffic GLBs and their offline preview are generated by the included original traffic generator. Existing Phase 1/2 procedural car/environment and synthesized sounds are retained. These are development assets, not a claim of final production audio quality.

Phase 4 adds original player GLBs from Tools/generate_player_models.py, explicit seven-role traffic materials, scenery GLBs from Tools/generate_environment.py, code-drawn steering wheel/contact shadow/asphalt effects, and four synthesized garage WAVs from Tools/generate_phase4_audio.py. No external branded models, photographs or sampled vehicle audio were used. Offline previews show geometry only and are not Godot screenshots.

## Phase 5 UI audio

`xp_gain.wav`, `level_up.wav`, `mission_complete.wav`, `reward_claim.wav`,
`achievement.wav` and `daily_reward.wav` are original deterministic sine/harmonic
chimes synthesized by `Tools/generate_phase5_audio.py`. Mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM.
No third-party recording, sample or melody was downloaded or copied. All existing
Phase 4 vehicle/environment assets and driving audio are retained.

## Phase 5 sports-visual fix
All 18 player LOD GLBs were authored with Tools/generate_sports_models.py from original parametric surfaces; no third-party model or texture was used. Tools/generate_player_models.py now delegates to that generator. NumPy is a generation dependency only; runtime uses embedded GLB assets. Docs/sports-lod-metrics.json supersedes historical player mesh metrics. The sports preview is an offline geometry projection, not a Godot render.

## Premium presentation pass
All revised player/Low traffic/scenery meshes, HDR panoramas, asphalt texture, UI SVG/pedal artwork, two Ogg compositions and 21 replacement sound effects are original code-generated work. No downloaded vehicle, texture, song, audio sample or proprietary font was used. Tools/generate_premium_surfaces.py, compose_premium_music.py and generate_premium_sfx.py document deterministic generation. Music master WAVs were temporary; only compressed Ogg tracks ship. The default Godot font remains in use.
